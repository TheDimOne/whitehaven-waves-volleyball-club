Who We Are

Volleyball is our
heartbeat

Founded in the coastal town of Whitehaven, the Waves are more than a volleyball team — we're a family forged by the tides. From grassroots beginnings on the sand to competing at regional level, our players bring the relentless energy of the ocean to every set, spike, and serve.

We believe in building athletes who are fearless on the court and grounded off it. Training hard, playing harder, and always showing up for each other — that's the Whitehaven way.
